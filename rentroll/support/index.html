Rent Roll support

Need help? Email support@bredbergmedia.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Please tell us:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version
  • the game version (shown in Settings, under About)
  • what happened, and what you expected
  • for a purchase problem, the order ID from Apple's receipt email

Never send card numbers or passwords. We'll never ask for them.

Purchases

I bought something and didn't get it.
Make sure you're online. Close the game fully, then open it again. Then go to Settings > Restore Purchases. Still missing? Email us the order ID.

How do I get a refund?
Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds ourselves.

What does Restore Purchases do?
It checks with the App Store for any purchase that hasn't reached your game yet, such as one a parent approved later with Ask to Buy, or one that was interrupted, and adds it. Then it tells you what it found. Empire Cash packs and the Starter Pack are used up when you buy them, so there's nothing else for the App Store to bring back.

Will Reset Everything take away what I paid for?
No. Reset Everything keeps unspent Empire Cash you bought, permanent Store unlocks and your settings. Everything else starts fresh.

I'm moving to a new iPhone.
Your save lives on your iPhone. If you set up the new iPhone from an iCloud backup or with Quick Start, the save comes along. Deleting the game deletes your save, including Empire Cash and Store unlocks you bought. Every purchase is used up when you buy it, so the App Store can't bring it back after a reinstall. Keep your iPhone backed up before you delete the game.

Income Sprint and ads

  • In the Store, tap Watch ad on the Income Sprint to watch a short ad and get 2× income for 15 minutes.
  • You can do this up to 3 times a day. Back-to-back sprints add up.
  • The daily count resets at midnight Pacific time. The game reads the time from the internet, so changing your phone's clock doesn't change it.
  • "No ad available": ads need an internet connection, and sometimes none is ready. Try again in a few minutes. A failed try doesn't count against your 3.
  • If you close an ad early, there's no sprint and the try isn't used up.
  • Ad privacy: use Settings > Ad Privacy Choices (it appears where your region's rules call for it). Rent Roll never asks to track you, so it has no switch in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. You get the sprint whatever you choose.
  • Saw an ad that seems wrong for this game? Email us a screenshot, with the time you saw it.

The game

  • Daily challenges and the login reward reset at midnight Pacific time.
  • To start over, go to Settings on the main menu > Reset Everything. This can't be undone.
  • Rent Roll runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
