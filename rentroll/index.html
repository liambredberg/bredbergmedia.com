Rent Roll: Real Estate Empire

An idle real estate game for iPhone

You inherited one empty house. Pick the tenants, buy the right deals, hold or flip, and grow it into a city of towers, hospitals and data centers.

  • Every vacancy brings offers. Safer tenants pay less; the ones who pay more are likelier to stop paying.
  • Hold or flip, take a loan or pay cash, and grow from one house to 25 kinds of buildings.
  • No pop-up ads. The only ad is one you choose to watch for an Income Sprint.

Coming soon to the App Store. iPhone, iOS 17 or later.
