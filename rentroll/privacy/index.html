Rent Roll privacy policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Rent Roll: Real Estate Empire ("Rent Roll", "the game") is made by Liam Bredberg (Bredberg Media) ("we", "us"). Questions: support@bredbergmedia.com.

The short version

  • No accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your save stays on your iPhone. We don't get a copy.
  • Apple handles every purchase. We never see your card or payment details.
  • The only ads are optional videos you choose to watch for an Income Sprint. Google AdMob provides them and collects some device data, described below.
  • The game never asks to track you, so Google never gets your iPhone's advertising identifier.
  • We use no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.
  • The game checks the time on the internet with Google and Cloudflare. Both see your IP address.
  • The game is not made for children under 13.

What stays on your iPhone

Your save holds your properties, cash, Empire Cash, what you've bought, achievements, daily progress and settings. It's kept in the game's private storage on your iPhone, and we never receive it.

If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, that backup can include the save. You and Apple control those backups, and we can't read them.

  • Reset Everything (in Settings on the main menu) erases your earned progress. Unspent Empire Cash you bought, permanent Store unlocks and your settings stay.
  • Deleting the game erases everything it stored on that iPhone.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple's App Store, and Apple handles your payment details under Apple's privacy policy. The game only learns which item was bought, so it can give it to you and record it in your save. We never see your name, card or Apple Account.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads in one place: after you tap Watch ad on the Store's Income Sprint. Nothing else in the game shows an ad.

Ads come from Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's consent tool (the User Messaging Platform). Each time the game opens, the consent tool asks Google which privacy messages apply where you are. Once your choices allow it, the ads SDK starts. It loads an ad when you open the Store or tap Watch ad.

Google says its ads SDK collects:

  • your IP address, used to estimate your general location
  • device identifiers limited to this app or developer. Not the advertising identifier (IDFA): the game never asks to track you, so iOS doesn't give it out
  • advertising data, such as which ads you've seen
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • performance data, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use
  • crash logs and other diagnostic data from the SDK

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its services. Google decides how long it keeps the data, under Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We see only totals in our AdMob reports, nothing that identifies you. Google's ads SDK is the only third-party code in the game that collects data. Google protects that data under its own policies linked above, which give at least the protection this policy describes.

Your choices about ads

  • No tracking. Rent Roll never asks to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites, so iOS never shows that question and keeps your advertising identifier from Google. The ads are less tailored to you as a result. The Income Sprint works the same for everyone.
  • Google's consent message. In the UK, the EEA, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, Google's message lets you choose how your data is used for ads. Where your region's rules call for it, you can change your choice in Rent Roll under Settings > Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Not watching. An ad only plays when you tap Watch ad.

The internet time check

The game reads the time from the internet, not from your phone's clock, so changing the clock can't reset the daily limits. It checks at launch, when you come back to the game, before each Income Sprint, and when you open the Store if the last check is more than 10 minutes old. If a check fails, it tries again every 30 seconds until one works.

It contacts two addresses:

  • connectivitycheck.gstatic.com (Google)
  • www.cloudflare.com (Cloudflare)

The game sends nothing about you, only a plain web request. Like any web server, these see your IP address and basic technical details of the request. The game reads only the time from the answer. Google and Cloudflare handle these requests under their own privacy policies (Google, Cloudflare).

Reminders

If you allow notifications, iOS schedules the game's reminders on your iPhone. Nothing goes to us or to any server. You can turn them off in Rent Roll's Settings or in iOS Settings > Notifications.

Crash reports from Apple

If you turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics about Rent Roll with us. Apple shares a limited set of this information. See Apple's App Analytics & Privacy page.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in, chat or player-made content.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or health data.
  • We don't sell your personal information for money. Google's use of data from its ads SDK to tailor ads may count as "selling" or "sharing" under California law. You can opt out in Settings > Ad Privacy Choices, as described above.

Children

Rent Roll is rated 13+ and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 used the game and Google's ads SDK collected their data, email support@bredbergmedia.com and we'll help.

How long data is kept

We run no servers, so we keep nothing. Your save stays on your device until you reset or delete the game. Google, Cloudflare and Apple keep data under their own policies.

Your rights in the US, including California

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information is collected about you
  • delete or correct it
  • opt out of its "sale," or its "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising

To opt out, use Settings > Ad Privacy Choices where it appears. The game never asks iOS for permission to track you, so iOS has no tracking switch for Rent Roll.

Using these rights changes nothing in the game. The Income Sprint and everything else work the same.

We don't hold personal information about you, so a request to see or delete your data usually finds nothing. If so, we'll tell you, and we'll point you to Google for data its SDK collected. Email support@bredbergmedia.com. We'll answer within 45 days.

Outside the US

If you're in the UK, you have rights under UK data protection law, such as the rights to access and delete your data, to object, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Personalized ads rely on the consent you give in Google's message, and you can withdraw it any time in Settings > Ad Privacy Choices.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects what data is collected, the update's release notes will say so too.
